🌪️ Types of Disasters

🌊 Flood

Move to higher ground and avoid flooded roads and bridges.

🌍 Earthquake

Drop, Cover and Hold until the shaking stops.

🔥 Fire

Leave the building safely and avoid using elevators.

🌀 Cyclone

Stay indoors and follow official emergency instructions.

🛡️ Safety Tips

  • Keep an emergency kit ready.
  • Store drinking water and essential food.
  • Keep a flashlight and extra batteries.
  • Save important emergency phone numbers.
  • Follow instructions from official authorities.
  • Do not spread fake or unverified information.
  • Help children, elderly people and injured persons.
